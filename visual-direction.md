# visual-direction.md

## 1. Purpose

This file defines the visual identity for `customer-app`.

The customer experience must feel:
- modern
- premium
- inviting
- trustworthy
- polished
- conversion-friendly

This document is the visual source of truth for layout feel, hierarchy, atmosphere, motion tone, and depth treatment.

---

## 2. Product Expression

`customer-app` is a commerce-facing experience.
It should help users:
- discover products easily
- trust the brand and platform
- browse comfortably
- act confidently
- complete conversion flows smoothly

The UI should feel richer and warmer than admin-web, but still disciplined and system-driven.

---

## 3. Design Philosophy

### Core principles
1. **Premium simplicity**
   - The UI should feel elevated, not crowded.
   - Visual polish should come from spacing, media, depth, and motion.

2. **Consistency across journeys**
   - Homepage, listing, product detail, cart, checkout, and account must feel like one family.

3. **Emotion with restraint**
   - The product can be expressive, but not chaotic.
   - Delight must never hurt clarity or conversion.

4. **Motion as brand and feedback**
   - Motion should add smoothness, confidence, and perceived quality.
   - Interactive feedback should feel tactile and intentional.

5. **Selective immersive depth**
   - Use gradients, media, blur, and optional 3D to create premium moments.
   - Keep the experience fast and usable.

---

## 4. Visual Keywords

Use these keywords:
- premium
- clean
- elegant
- modern
- inviting
- smooth
- curated
- layered
- trustworthy
- conversion-focused

Avoid:
- cluttered promotion-heavy layouts
- random bright color blocks
- inconsistent card styles
- excessive novelty UI
- noisy visual backgrounds
- motion that fights the content

---

## 5. Layout Direction

### Page character
Customer pages should feel modular, breathable, and scroll-friendly.

### Rules
- Use clear section rhythm from top to bottom.
- Hero and featured zones can be visually richer.
- Product cards and CTA areas must remain consistent.
- Browsing and purchase flows must feel easy and calm.

### Preferred patterns
- hero + category/collection sections
- product grid + filter/sort
- media-first product detail
- sticky conversion summary where useful
- modular content blocks with strong spacing rhythm

---

## 6. Surface and Depth Style

### Surface approach
The UI may use:
- clean light or dark-neutral base surfaces
- elevated cards
- subtle gradients in hero/promo zones
- selective blur for premium overlays

### Depth approach
Depth can come from:
- soft layered shadows
- elevated cards
- image framing
- gentle gradient lighting
- selective glass-like overlays in premium sections

Avoid excessive glassmorphism or over-stylized effects across the entire app.

---

## 7. Color Direction

### Base strategy
- Neutral surfaces should maintain clarity and consistency.
- Brand color should guide CTA hierarchy and interaction states.
- Promotional accents may be richer than admin, but still controlled.

### Guidelines
- Use gradients intentionally for hero and featured storytelling.
- Reserve semantic colors for system states and trust messaging.
- Large promotional sections may use accent-tinted surfaces, but body browsing areas should remain calm.

---

## 8. Typography Direction

Typography should communicate:
- trust
- polish
- readability
- warmth
- modernity

### Rules
- Headlines can be more expressive than admin, but must remain clean.
- Product and price hierarchy must be obvious.
- Long descriptions must stay easy to read.
- Promotional text should never overpower the actual product journey.

---

## 9. Shape Language

### Corners
Use modern rounded corners consistently.

### Guidance
- chips/inputs: smaller radius
- buttons/cards: medium to large radius
- hero or premium modules: larger radius allowed

### Borders
Borders should remain subtle.
Use them for structure, not decoration.

---

## 10. Component Feel

Components should feel:
- tactile
- polished
- responsive
- cohesive
- premium

### Buttons
- clear CTA hierarchy
- comfortable interaction targets
- premium hover and press feedback

### Cards
- image-first where appropriate
- strong alignment and content rhythm
- hover and elevation only when useful

### Inputs
- clean and confidence-building
- especially important in auth, cart, and checkout flows

---

## 11. Motion Direction

### Motion style
Use:
- smooth fade
- soft translate
- subtle scale
- controlled hover lift
- image/content transition polish
- section reveal in moderation

### Motion goals
- make browsing feel premium
- reinforce quality and responsiveness
- improve continuity between states
- support delight in non-critical moments

### Avoid
- excessive bouncing
- overloaded parallax
- simultaneous animation on every section
- distracting motion in checkout-critical flows

---

## 12. 3D Direction

3D is optional but more acceptable here than in admin.

### Suitable uses
- homepage hero
- featured campaign section
- product storytelling block
- premium product showcase
- brand story section

### Rules
- 3D must remain lightweight
- it must not block content or conversion
- it must degrade gracefully
- it should feel elegant, not gimmicky

---

## 13. Accessibility Expectations

- Strong contrast for text and controls
- Clear focus states
- Motion must support reduced-motion preferences
- Key states must not rely on color alone
- Checkout and cart flows must remain highly legible

---

## 14. Final Standard

A strong customer screen should feel:
- premium but usable
- modern but stable
- expressive but consistent
- immersive in the right places
- easy to browse and easy to buy

When in doubt:
- choose clarity over visual spectacle
- choose cohesion over variety
- choose tasteful depth over heavy effects
