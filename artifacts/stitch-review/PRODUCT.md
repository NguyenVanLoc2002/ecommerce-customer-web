# Fashion Shop review context

## Register
product

## Users
Vietnamese CUSTOMER shoppers browse on mobile and compare clothing on desktop, often in daylight. A warm light canvas and clear contrast support this context.

## Product Purpose
Discover clothing, understand price and catalog variants, select an exact purchasable combination and continue through the existing authenticated cart.

## Brand Personality
Quiet, considered, approachable. Keep Fashion Shop identity. Photos and useful product information lead. Be Vietnam Pro throughout.

## Anti-references
No ornate serif, SaaS card grids, gradients, oversized pills, fake scarcity/reviews, unverified shipping promises or promotional overlays.

## Design Principles
1. Images invite exploration; hierarchy clarifies purchase decisions.
2. Show only choices supported by the catalog contract.
3. Distinguish unknown stock, unavailable combinations and server stock failure.
4. Mobile supports the same decisions as desktop.
5. Preview artifacts stay isolated until approval.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Vietnamese diacritics, wrapping long names, semantic controls, visible focus, 44px touch targets, status announcements, keyboard drawers and reduced motion. Target WCAG AA contrast and record checks.
